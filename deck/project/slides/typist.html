Two ways to visit a million Roman camps

Send Obelix, or send Asterix with the potion?

Obelix · the agent with files + grep

  • greps for the pills, then improvises a regex script, a new one every time
  • bangs it against the compiler until it stops complaining: 8–17 turns
  • reads 251–652 k tokens on the way
  • gets there: correct in 8 of 8 runs

Asterix · the agent with ng-facts

  • thinks first: reads the v2 API, once
  • describes the change in four lines
  • drinks the potion: the compiler applies 2 680 edits in 0.1–0.2 s
  • 3–6 turns, 96–165 k tokens, unless he trips; correct in 8 of 8

Both use the compiler. Obelix as the referee after the fight. Asterix as the map before it.

Be fair to Obelix: in the recordings he never types the edits by hand, he writes a sed or perl script and iterates on the ngc errors, and he ends correct every time. What differs is how much context he burns getting there. Numbers are min–max over 8 runs per lane.

Both work through a script. A script does not care how many files.

The same mission at three sizes: 30, 120 and 480 components. Every run was correct. We expected Obelix's bill to grow with the app. It did not: he writes a script, and a script does not care how many files it runs over. Asterix's bill grows a little: six cents at 30 components, seven cents for his clean run at 480. His dearer runs are the two where a shell command of his own hung. So the potion buys a smaller bill at every size, not a different growth curve.

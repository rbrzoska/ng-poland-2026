The first tool worked, but the recordings showed wasted trips: asking for the format, writing the recipe to a file, running the compiler again. So we changed three small things, and put the exact call into the prompt. Five runs of five were right, each in two trips to the model, for five cents. The lesson: say exactly how to call the script, and the run gets cheaper.

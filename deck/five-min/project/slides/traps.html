These four lines are real lines from the test app. A plain find and replace gets no line right and leaves all 700 compiler errors. A careful regex gets about a third of the lines right and leaves 220 errors. These three are scripts, with no agent. Obelix does get out of the traps: he tries again until the compiler is silent, and those tries are what he pays for. The compiler's parser does not guess. It knows which element is which, so the potion gets 640 of 640.
